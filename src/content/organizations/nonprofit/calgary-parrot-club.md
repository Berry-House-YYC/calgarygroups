The Calgary Parrot Club was founded in June 1988 and exists to inform and educate on the subject of companion parrots and to provide camaraderie, education and friendship for members in a fun, respectful and judgement-free environment.

The Calgary Parrot Club also hosts fundraisers such as the annual Parrot Palooza, where you can meet birds and fellow bird-lovers, and support animal rescue organizations.
