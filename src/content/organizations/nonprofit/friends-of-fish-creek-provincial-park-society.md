Their vision is a sustainable Fish Creek Provincial Park that improves the quality of life for present and future generations. Their mission is to engage the community through activities and awareness to conserve a truly  unique naturalized urban park. They host educational events, community programs, and offer volunteering opportunities on environmental topics like watershed stewardship, conservation of native plants, wildlife monitoring, and birding.
